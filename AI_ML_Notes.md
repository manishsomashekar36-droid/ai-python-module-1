# Introduction to AI & Python – Module 1

## 1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:
- Voice assistants
- Chatbots
- Recommendation systems
- Self-driving vehicles
- Face recognition

## 2. What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI where computers learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every situation.

Examples:
- Spam email detection
- Movie recommendations
- Fraud detection
- Predicting house prices

## 3. AI vs ML vs Deep Learning

### Artificial Intelligence
The broad concept of making machines intelligent.

### Machine Learning
A subset of AI where machines learn from data.

### Deep Learning
A subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.

Relationship:

AI → Machine Learning → Deep Learning

## 4. Real-World Applications of AI

- Healthcare – disease detection and medical image analysis
- Banking – fraud detection
- Education – personalized learning
- Transportation – autonomous vehicles
- Entertainment – content recommendations
- Agriculture – crop monitoring
- Customer service – AI chatbots
